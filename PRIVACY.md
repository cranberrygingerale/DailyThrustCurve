# Privacy Notice

_Last updated: October 13, 2026_

Thrust Curve Daily ("the game") is a free browser game hosted on GitHub Pages at https://cranberrygingerale.github.io/DailyThrustCurve/. This notice explains what happens to your information when you play.

**The short version:** we don't collect, see or store anything about you. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, tracking pixels or cookies. Your progress stays in your own browser.

## What the game stores on your device

The game uses your browser's local storage (not cookies) to remember:

- which daily puzzle you're on and the guesses you've made today
- whether you've finished today's puzzle
- your stats: games played, wins, current streak, best streak and the day of your last win
- whether you've already seen the "How to play" panel

This information never leaves your device. We can't see it and it isn't sent anywhere. Practice rounds aren't saved. To erase everything, clear this site's data in your browser settings.

This storage is strictly necessary for the game to do what you ask, like keeping your streak, so no consent banner is required.

## Third parties involved in loading the page

When you open the game, your browser connects to these services. Like any web request, each connection shares your IP address and basic browser information with that service:

- **GitHub Pages** hosts the game. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes, whether or not you have a GitHub account. See [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
- **Google Fonts** serves the game's typefaces. See [Google Fonts' privacy FAQ](https://developers.google.com/fonts/faq/privacy) and [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

We don't receive or control the data these services collect.

## Other things the game does

- **Copy result** writes your result grid to your clipboard. The game never reads your clipboard.
- **External links**, such as "See this motor on ThrustCurve.org", take you to other websites with their own privacy practices.

## Children

The game doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes

If this notice changes, the new version will be posted in the game's [GitHub repository](https://github.com/cranberrygingerale/DailyThrustCurve) with an updated date. You can view earlier versions in the repository's history.
